    How the app decides

    Each chart's sign map comes from BPHS house logic: lagna, 3rd, 9th, 10th and 11th signs are clear; 8th from lagna, 8th from the Moon and the 12th are avoided; other signs suit only their own house topics. Sign nature (chara, sthira, dvisvabhava) and rising type follow BPHS chapter 4.

    The day filters come from the muhurta tradition: Chandrabala (Moon good in 1, 3, 6, 7, 10, 11 from your Moon; 2, 5, 9 in the bright half; Chandrashtama is the 8th), Tarabala from Purva Ashadha, panchaka (tithi + weekday + nakshatra + lagna, divided by 9), Rahu kalam, Yamaganda, Gulika, Bhadra (Vishti karana), Vyatipata and Vaidhriti yogas, and lagna gandanta (12 minutes either side of the Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces junctions). Ghatak items are the ones you enter for each profile, usually from a printed report.

    A new profile's chart is calculated from date, time and place. Ashtakavarga is computed from the BPHS bindu tables; for the built-in chart it matches the AstroSage printout cell for cell.

    Positions are computed in the page itself, with no internet needed. Checked against the Astrodienst 2026 ephemeris and your AstroSage chart: Sun and Moon within about a minute of arc, planets within about 0.1°, times within a minute. Dasha dates follow AstroSage's calendar arithmetic, so they match your printout.
